SUTine
by Yojana

Sutíne didn't come from a perfect plan. It came from wanting back the softness — that ghar jasto feel.

As a Nepali woman, Yojana grew up around soft cotton, light fabrics and clothes made for comfort. So she stopped searching and started creating.

Every piece is made slowly, by hand, by a small team of skilled women in Nepal. Simple, but intentional. Nothing is rushed.

Our first collection blends culture and comfort — inspired by the traditional chaubandi choli, with a modern touch.

Handcrafted with love in Nepal
