Pop-up stall · print guide

Friday 6 & Saturday 7 November

How the stall comes together

  1. Back left: A1 banner on its frame, slightly angled so it can be seen from the walkway.
  2. Table: plain beige or white base cloth to the floor, A3 runner on top, flowers facing the customer. Use the A2 banner instead of the runner if the table is wider than 1.5 m.
  3. On the table: B1 Follow us + B2 Our story in wooden block stands, folded pieces in low stacks, Totes & Thailis in a basket.
  4. Rail: on the right, wooden hangers, light to dark. B4 tag on every piece, B3 "Try it on" sign clipped to the end.
  5. Bags: kraft bag, cream tissue sealed with a B6 sticker, and a B5 thank-you card inside.

Notes for the printer

  • Files are vector PDFs at 100% finished size. Fabric: add 3 cm hem allowance on every side (no bleed is built in). Card: print edge to edge, then trim on the dashed guides.
  • Colours: cream #F7F3EC, maroon #7A1F26, olive #5F6B3A, ink #2A2523. Ask for a small test swatch of the fabric. Maroon goes darker on canvas.
  • If you print on natural or undyed linen, the cream background can be left unprinted so the fabric shows through.
  • Scan every QR code on the proof before the full run.
